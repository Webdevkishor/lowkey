Put product photos here, named with the product code, e.g. LM-SH-01.png

Then point the product at it in js/products.js:
  image: "assets/products/LM-SH-01.png"

Best look: a cut-out PNG (background removed, no hanger). The site trims
empty space around it and hangs it from its own wooden hanger in 3D.
A normal JPG also works and shows as a picture.

About 1000-1400 px on the long side; JPG under ~200 KB, PNG under ~500 KB.
Until a photo is set, the site draws the garment itself.
